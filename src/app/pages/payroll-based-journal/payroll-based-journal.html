ACA – PAYROLL BASED JOURNAL

Product News | July 1, 2016

Jantek is pleased to announce a time & attendance solution for Nursing Homes to meet the ACA Payroll-Based Journal reporting requirement.

According to the Affordable Care Act (ACA): As of July 1, 2016 all nursing homes will be required to use PBJ and file staff and census reports regularly.

Please contact marketing@jantek.com for a free quote on a new time attendance system with PBJ report export utility for nursing homes or long term care facilities.

Existing nursing homes with Jantek time & attendance system can also contact Jantek for this PBJ report software upgrade.
