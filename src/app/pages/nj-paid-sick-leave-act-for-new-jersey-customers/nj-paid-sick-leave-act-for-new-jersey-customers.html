NJ PAID SICK LEAVE ACT (FOR NEW JERSEY CUSTOMERS)

Product News | October 29, 2018

New Jersey Paid Sick Leave Act - effective Oct. 29, 2018:

Employees will accrue one hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours per year. All New Jersey employers must provide paid sick leave to their employees.
