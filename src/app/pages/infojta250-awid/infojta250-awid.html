JTA250-AWID TIME CLOCK FOR AWID CARDS

The JTA250-AWID Time Clock is a perfect time clock for employees with AWID access cards. The JTA250-AWID Proximity Time Clock is an excellent solution that allows companies to take advantage of automated time and attendance. Not only is the terminal sleek and compact, it is a time clock with AWID reader built-in that stresses ease-of-use.

Employees simply wave their AWID badges in front of the terminal to punch IN or OUT, even when the badge is in the employee's wallet or purse. With AWID card time clock integration, no direct contact is required and there are no moving parts making the terminal virtually maintenance free. This AWID time tracking solution has two software programmable function keys that allow for department or job transfers, entry of tips, or the viewing of the employee's last punch.

The JTA250-AWID access control time clock features public and private messaging that allow management to send messages via the clock to all, or to selected employees. Optional features include lockout times when employees are prevented from punching IN or OUT, bell ringing, and opening of doors.

Time Clock Features

✓

Internal proximity card reader.

✓

✓

Retains last 6000 punches.

✓

Battery backup for date & time.

✓

✓

Built-in Daylight Saving Time.

✓

✓

Two programmable function keys that can be set for:

  • Entering tips
✓

Programmable bell schedule for ringing electric bells (requires bell relay kit) - each day of the week can have 50 different ring times.

✓

Public and private messages.

✓

Optional lockout feature for preventing early punch-in and late punch-out (prevents unauthorized overtime) - internal schedule holds 2 week lockout profile for each employee.
