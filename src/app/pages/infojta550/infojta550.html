FACESCAN JTA-550

FaceSCAN is a truly embedded facial recognition system designed for Access Control, Time & Attendance, and User Authentication applications. Powered by advanced video capturing, system design and Near-Infrared (NIR) facial recognition technologies, FaceSCAN provides superior accuracy and reliability over traditional facial recognition systems under different light conditions.

Features Highlights

USER-FRIENDLY AND ULTRA COMPACT

  • ✓The LCD on-screen display provides simple and interactive instructions guiding users to position their faces during authentication. NO prior user training is necessary.
  • ✓The JTA-550 is the size of a smart phone and only 0.8" thick when wall mounted.

DUAL SPECTRUM RECOGNITION

  • ✓The JTA-550 performs dual spectrum recognition with both Infrared (IR) and visible light. Works under total darkness, and various lighting conditions.

LIVENESS DETECTION

  • ✓Rejects hacking with photos, video image, and even realistic facial masks.

SPEEDY RECOGNITION

  • ✓Facial recognition in less than 1 second.
