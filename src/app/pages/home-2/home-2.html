Jantek Electronics

JANTEK ELECTRONICS, INC. is an established manufacturer of time management and security access systems with corporate offices located in Temple City, California. For over 20 years, we have provided time and attendance and shop floor control systems, access control systems and security related products to private industries and government institutions. We have also produced OEM and licensed products for major corporations on a partnership basis.
