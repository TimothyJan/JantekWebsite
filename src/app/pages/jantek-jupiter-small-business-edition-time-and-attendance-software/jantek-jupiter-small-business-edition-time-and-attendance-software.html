SMALL BUSINESS EDITION TIMEKEEPING

Small Business Edition of the popular Time & Attendance software

Jupiter 9.0 SX is a simplified version of the Jupiter 9.0 Time & Attendance software for 50 employees (100 employee version optional). When purchased in a package with the proximity reader time clock, it includes 25 badges, wall mount, 50-foot communication cable, 2 keys and a time clock operation manual.

Also available is a software-only package where employees register their attendance from a "virtual" time clock program on their PC workstation, or a PC dedicated to this purpose (see PC Punch under the Add-on Modules section for more details on the features of this option).

With whichever option you choose, the Jupiter 9.0 SX software contains a Quick Start Guide that guides you to set up the system to suit your company's needs, a Daily Procedures section that reminds you of the suggested daily tasks, and a Newly Hired Employee section that provides the steps to add a new hire into the system.
