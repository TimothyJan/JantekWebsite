PC PUNCH

With PC Punch, no additional time clock hardware, cabling, or cards are needed. Employees are able to punch from a PC with an easy-to-use, customizable virtual time clock program.

Programmable features include setting the "function" buttons to perform level changes, earning/deduction entry, view last punch, and review total hours for the current pay period.

Software Features

✓

Employees can punch IN and OUT from their own workstations or from a dedicated PC.

✓

Eliminates cabling and fussing with cords.

✓

Utilizes server's date and time to avoid any misrepresentation of punches.

✓

Designed to operate under Windows (7, 8, 8.1, 10 and 11) and Windows network environments (optionally Linux with Samba).
