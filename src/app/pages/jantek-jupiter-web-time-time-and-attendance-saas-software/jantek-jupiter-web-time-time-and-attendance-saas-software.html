JANTEK WEB TIME – CLOUD-BASED TIME AND ATTENDANCE SAAS SOLUTION PRODUCT RELEASE

Product News | July 27, 2016

Product News:

July 27, 2016

Jantek is proud to announce the release of Web Time, their cloud-based time and attendance / workforce management Software as a Service (SaaS) solution. Contact us to learn how you can automate your workforce with Jantek's Web Time SaaS solution to increase productivity and streamline labor force management. The subscription-based time and attendance software service removes the need to purchase licenses, software installation and ongoing system updates. Timekeeping made easy using our Web Time: it is a web-based solution allowing you access to the workforce management data anytime, anywhere, with an internet connection.
